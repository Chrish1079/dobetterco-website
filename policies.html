Privacy policy

Do Better Co. (“we,” “us,” “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you visit this website, join our email list, or place an order, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Email list: If you subscribe through our site, we collect the email address you submit via our signup form (hosted by Netlify).
  • Orders: When you buy through our Stripe checkout links, payment and fulfillment details (name, email, shipping address, payment method) are collected and processed by Stripe. We receive order information needed to fulfill your purchase (such as items ordered and shipping details).
  • Usage data: Our host may collect standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages viewed) for security and performance.

How we use information

We use your information to send updates you opted into, fulfill orders, respond to requests, improve our site, and comply with law. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share data with service providers that help us operate the business, including Stripe (payments) and Netlify (forms and hosting). They process data under their own privacy policies. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and customers.

Cookies

Our static site may use minimal session or security-related cookies from our host. Stripe checkout may use cookies when you complete a purchase on Stripe’s pages.

Children

Our site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any message. For order-related data processed by Stripe, you may also contact Stripe directly. To request access, correction, or deletion of information we hold, email support@dobetterco.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change.

Refund policy

We want you to be happy with your order. If something is not right, contact us and we will work with you on a fair resolution.

Eligible refunds & exchanges

  • Defective or damaged items received (with photo proof within 7 days of delivery).
  • Wrong item or size shipped by us.
  • Orders lost in transit after carrier investigation (when applicable).

Non-refundable items

  • Items that show wear, washing, or damage after delivery.
  • Change-of-mind returns on limited drops, unless required by law.
  • Final-sale items marked as such at checkout.

Time frame

Contact us within 14 days of delivery for eligible issues. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method through Stripe; timing depends on your bank or card issuer (typically 5–10 business days after we process the refund).

How to start a return or refund

Email support@dobetterco.com with your order number (from your Stripe receipt), a description of the issue, and photos if the item is damaged or incorrect. We will reply with return instructions when a return is required.

Return shipping

If we made an error or the product is defective, we cover return shipping. For other approved exchanges, return shipping may be your responsibility unless otherwise stated.

Shipping policy

We ship physical orders from the United States. Shipping options and costs appear at Stripe checkout.

Orders typically ship within 3–7 business days after payment clears. During new drops or busy periods, processing may take longer; we will post updates when possible.

We ship to addresses within the United States. Estimated delivery after shipment is usually 3–7 business days for standard service, depending on carrier and destination. Tracking is provided by email when available.
